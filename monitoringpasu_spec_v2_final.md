## PASS 1: CZECH COPY REVIEW

### Issues found in v1.0 and corrections:

**Problem 1: "0 Kč" in USP**
"Za 5 minut práce a 0 Kč" is awkward. Czech readers scan "0 Kč" as a price tag, not a benefit. The word "zdarma" is natural, human, and universally understood.

**Problem 2: Roller stats don't belong**
The three stat callouts about rollers (75% poruch, 10% zablokovaných válečků, požáry) describe MCube RFP, not MCube CAM. MCube CAM is about the belt surface — splices, edge damage, surface wear, anomalies. The stats must speak to belt-specific pain.

**Problem 3: "neinspektujeme" is not Czech**
The word doesn't exist. The correct form would be "neprovádíme inspekce" or simply "kontroly neprovádíme pravidelně." The AI consultant question options need rewriting into natural spoken Czech.

**Problem 4: "Chcete takovou zprávu pro vaše pásy?" is stilted**
"Pro vaše pásy" is technically correct but reads like a translation. A Czech maintenance manager would say "pro své pásy" or more naturally, the sentence should be restructured entirely.

**Problem 5: Overall tone is too "marketingový"**
Czech industrial professionals are allergic to marketing-speak. Neuron Soundware gets away with it because they target innovation managers and VCs. Our ICP is a 50-year-old hlavní mechanik v Mostě who's been vulcanizing belts since 1995. The copy needs to be direct, factual, and slightly blunt — like an engineer explaining something to another engineer over coffee.

### REVISED COPY — Section by Section

#### SECTION 1: HERO

**H1:** "Víte, jak na tom vaše pásy jsou?"
*(Do you know what condition your belts are in?)*
— Short. Direct question. No fluff. "Jak na tom jsou" is natural spoken Czech.

**Subheadline:** "Natočte pás mobilem. Umělá inteligence vyhodnotí poškození, která pouhým okem neuvidíte. Zprávu s mapou defektů dostanete do 5 pracovních dnů. První diagnostika zdarma."
*(Film the belt with your phone. AI evaluates damage invisible to the naked eye. You'll receive a defect map report within 5 business days. First diagnostic free.)*

Changes from v1:
- "chytrým telefonem" → "mobilem" (that's what Czech people actually say)
- "AI najde poškození, která lidské oko přehlédne" → "Umělá inteligence vyhodnotí poškození, která pouhým okem neuvidíte" — more specific (evaluates, not just "finds"), uses "pouhým okem" which is a standard Czech technical phrase
- Added "s mapou defektů" — gives the reader a concrete image of the output
- "první diagnostika zdarma" not "0 Kč"

**CTA:** "Chci bezplatnou diagnostiku"
*(I want a free diagnostic)*
— First person ("chci") is stronger than imperative ("získejte"). The reader mentally commits by clicking "I want."

**Trust bar:** "REMA TIP TOP — přes 100 let zkušeností v údržbě dopravníkových systémů | Pobočky v Brně a Praze"
— "údržba dopravníkových systémů" mirrors VVV MOST's language ("servisní činnost pro dopravní pásy")

## PASS 2: UX FLOW REVIEW

### Current flow assessment:

The page needs to guide two distinct user types to two distinct actions. The risk is that Path B users (procurement) bounce because they can't find the PDF price list without scrolling through the AI chat. And Path A users (maintenance) bounce because they see 9 sections of content before reaching the form.

### Revised flow architecture:

**Above the fold (Section 1 — Hero):**
- H1 + subheadline + CTA "Chci bezplatnou diagnostiku" (scrolls to form)
- Second smaller link: "Stáhnout ceník (PDF)" — visible immediately, no scrolling needed. This catches Path B users before they bounce.

**Below the fold, in order of priority for conversion:**

Section 2 (Problem/Solution) — Validates the reader's pain. "Yes, this is about me."
Section 3 (How It Works) — Removes complexity objection. "Oh, it's that simple?"
Section 4 (Sample Report) — The closer. "That's what I'd get? Impressive."
Section 5 (Lead Capture Form) — The conversion point. Two paths clearly separated.
Section 6 (Pricing) — Addresses the "what does it cost" question. Builds trust.
Section 7 (Value Proposition) — Reinforcement for those still scrolling.
Section 8 (Trust Signals) — Final credibility push.
Section 9 (Footer) — Contact info.

### Key UX decisions:

**Sticky CTA bar on mobile:** Once the user scrolls past the hero, a thin sticky bar appears at bottom with "Chci bezplatnou diagnostiku" button. Always one tap away from the form.

**Form design:** Single-column layout. Group fields into two clearly labeled sections: "O vás" (About you — 5 fields) and "O pásu" (About the belt — 5 fields + video upload). Progress indicator showing "Krok 1 ze 2" if needed. Submit button: "Odeslat a získat diagnostiku zdarma."

**Video upload UX:** Large drag-and-drop zone with clear messaging: "Přetáhněte video sem nebo klikněte pro výběr souboru. Přijímáme .mp4 a .mov do 5 GB." Show upload progress bar. After upload completes, show green checkmark + filename + file size.

**Technician visit path:** Not hidden. Equal visual weight as "upload yourself." Two cards side by side:
- Card 1: "Nahraji video sám/sama" → expands to full form
- Card 2: "Chci návštěvu technika" → simplified form (name, company, email, phone, site, preferred date range)

**Sample report:** NOT a PDF download. Embedded image carousel or lightbox showing 3-4 key pages: the belt map overview, a close-up defect photo with severity rating, the summary table. The user should see the quality without leaving the page.

## PASS 3: CONVERSION OPTIMIZATION

### Identified churn points and fixes:

**Churn point 1: Hero → scroll**
Risk: User reads headline, doesn't understand what MCube CAM is, bounces.
Fix: The subheadline now explains the full value chain in one sentence: film → AI → report → free. Nothing left unexplained. The CTA "Chci bezplatnou diagnostiku" is in first person — psychologically, the user begins to self-identify as someone who wants this.

**Churn point 2: "Sounds interesting but I need to see what I'd get"**
Risk: User is intrigued but won't commit without evidence.
Fix: Sample report section moved to Section 4 (before the form, not after). The user sees the output quality BEFORE being asked to submit. This is the most important anti-churn decision on the page.

**Churn point 3: Form abandonment**
Risk: User starts filling the form, hits "rychlost pásu (m/s)" and thinks "I don't know off the top of my head," abandons.
Fix: 
- Add helper text under each technical field: e.g., "Rychlost pásu najdete v dokumentaci dopravníku nebo na štítku pohonu. Pokud neznáte přesnou hodnotu, uveďte odhad — ověříme společně."
- Make the video upload field visually dominant (large drop zone) — it's the most important input. Technical fields are secondary.
- Add a note above the technical section: "Nemáte všechny údaje po ruce? Vyplňte, co znáte — zbytek doplníme při krátkém telefonátu."
  *(Don't have all the details handy? Fill in what you know — we'll complete the rest in a short phone call.)*
  This single line prevents form abandonment from incomplete data.

**Churn point 4: "I don't have time to film right now"**
Risk: User is convinced but filming requires planning (safety permit, scheduled stop, etc.).
Fix: The "Chci návštěvu technika" path is visible at equal weight. AND below the form, add: "Plánujete odstávku v nejbližších týdnech? Domluvte si diagnostiku předem — budete mít výsledky hned po restartu."
*(Planning a shutdown in the coming weeks? Schedule the diagnostic in advance — you'll have results right after restart.)*
This reframes the timing from "do it now" to "plan it for your next stop."

**Churn point 5: "Free sounds suspicious"**
Risk: Czech industrial buyer doesn't trust "zdarma."
Fix: Already addressed in pricing section with explicit explanation: "Chceme, abyste viděli kvalitu zprávy, než se rozhodnete." But reinforce in the form area with a micro-copy line: "Žádné skryté poplatky, žádné automatické objednávky. Zprávu dostanete a rozhodnutí je na vás."
*(No hidden fees, no automatic orders. You receive the report and the decision is yours.)*

**Churn point 6: Procurement manager leaves without converting**
Risk: Path B user can't find what they need (price list, references) and bounces.
Fix: 
- "Stáhnout ceník (PDF)" link visible in hero section (no scrolling needed)
- Pricing table in Section 6 is ungated and prominent
- "Domluvit schůzku" button in Path B leads to a 4-field form (name, company, email, phone) — minimal friction
- Add a single line for this persona in Section 8: "Podklady pro interní schvalování (ceník, reference, technická specifikace) zašleme na vyžádání."
  *(Materials for internal approval (price list, references, technical specification) available on request.)*

**Churn point 7: Mobile users**
Risk: Maintenance managers may access from a phone on-site (checking during a break, shared link from a colleague).
Fix: 
- Sticky bottom CTA bar on mobile
- Form fields large enough for thumb input
- Video upload supports "take video" directly from phone camera (not just file picker)
- Page loads under 2 seconds on 3G

### Conversion funnel summary:

```
VISIT
  ├─ Hero: understand problem + see "zdarma" → CTA or scroll
  ├─ Problem/Solution: "this is me" validation → keep scrolling
  ├─ How It Works: "I can do that" → keep scrolling
  ├─ Sample Report: "that's impressive" → ready to convert
  │
  ├─ FORM (Path A)
  │   ├─ Upload video path → full form → SUBMIT
  │   └─ Technician visit path → short form → SUBMIT
  │
  └─ INFO (Path B)
      ├─ Download price list PDF → offline evaluation → return later
      └─ Request meeting → short form → SUBMIT
```

### Anti-churn elements embedded throughout:

- First-person CTAs ("Chci..." not "Získejte...")
- Sample report BEFORE the form
- "Nemáte všechny údaje? Nevadí." messaging at every friction point
- Equal visibility for "film yourself" and "we'll come to you"
- Sticky mobile CTA
- No email gates on any content (price list, sample report)
- Explicit "no catch" messaging near the free offer
- Planning-oriented CTA for those not ready to act today
